---
title: Reporting a vulnerability
weight: 9
---

Report vulnerabilities in Tephra privately through
[GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/tephrasec/tephra/security/advisories/new), not in public
issues. You will get an answer, and credit if you want it, when the fix is released.

The machine-readable contact is at [/.well-known/security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt).
